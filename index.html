YOUR PREFERENCES

Settings & backup

Personalize FINOVA, protect your local data and manage your saved information.

Appearance

Amounts are stored as entered; this setting does not convert currencies.

Backup & restore

Export your FINOVA records to a JSON file. Keep your backup somewhere safe. Importing a backup replaces the current local app data.

Data is stored in this browser using local storage. Browser clearing, private browsing or device changes may remove or separate your data. Export backups regularly.

About FINOVA

FINOVA is a personal finance tracker designed to help you understand your money. Figures depend on the records you enter. It does not connect to banks, retrieve live investments, process real card payments or provide regulated financial advice.
